Title: BASS/BASS-II SIBAL composite fabric flame tests

Evidence summary from the NASA NTRS record and paper (Document ID 20150008962): NASA studied a composite cotton-fiberglass fabric called SIBAL aboard the International Space Station. The report describes 27 BASS-II tests for the fabric, including 3 non-ignited tests, 6 quenching tests, and 1 blow-off test. The tests examined flame growth, spread, and extinction at different oxygen concentrations and forced-flow speeds. The source reports oxygen from 16% to 21% and flow speeds from 1 cm/s to 40 cm/s. The study does not mean that every cotton fabric behaves like this composite sample.

Material mapping note: “pamuklu kumaş” in the demo is a clearly disclosed alias for the SIBAL cotton-fiberglass composite, not a claim about pure cotton.
