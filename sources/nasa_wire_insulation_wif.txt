Title: USML-1 Wire Insulation Flammability (WIF)

Evidence summary from NASA NTRS Document ID 19960008389: The WIF experiment studied 0.65 mm polyethylene insulation on wire in a reduced-gravity flight experiment. Four tests were conducted (WIF A–D), in concurrent and opposed flow. The report states that ignition was achieved in all four tests; the flames were bright and produced soot. The samples were tested in approximately 21% oxygen at 1 atm. WIF-C's flame did not ultimately propagate; it initially ignited and burned weakly, then failed to propagate after airflow was started.
