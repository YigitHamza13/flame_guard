Title: Forced Flow Flame-spreading Test (FFFT), NASA-Mir Phase 1 Science Report

Evidence summary: NASA's Phase 1 Science Report describes the FFFT tests aboard Mir. Four flat cellulose samples of differing thickness and four polyethylene wire-insulation samples were used. It states that all samples successfully ignited and burned in very low-speed airflow. This card uses the four flat cellulose samples as the source match for the input “kağıt”; it does not generalize to all paper products.
